# Christmas gifts 2026

A personal Christmas gift-planning dashboard for finding products, comparing offers, tracking prices, planning a budget, and deciding whether a verified deal is worth buying before Black Friday.

The site keeps each signed-in person's gift list private and stores gifts, groups, budgets, offer snapshots, observed price history, and in-app deal alerts in a durable database. Generic store links use standards-based product metadata when available. Broad product discovery is designed for SerpApi's Google Shopping data and transparently degrades until a `SERPAPI_KEY` is configured.

## Current capabilities

- Product-idea search and direct product-link tracking
- Best Deal scoring based on total known cost, product match, seller reliability, deal strength, delivery context, and freshness
- Observed price history and significant price-drop alerts
- Purchase status, editable family groups, and an overall budget
- Budget-impact and verified alternative-savings recommendations
- Annual U.S. Black Friday countdown calculated from Thanksgiving
- Responsive, accessible interface with ChatGPT sign-in

The app does not claim universal retailer coverage. Stores may block automated access, expose ambiguous product variants, or omit reliable prices. Shipping, tax, condition, and checkout totals must be verified at the seller.
